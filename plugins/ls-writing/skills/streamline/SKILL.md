---
name: streamline
description: Use when content is verbose, repetitive, or padded and needs tightening while preserving meaning
---

If the TARGET of this command is not already clear from the context, ask the user: "What would you like to streamline?" Substitute TARGET for the user's response below.

Analyze TARGET to identify and remove redundant, verbose, or low-value content.

Test every sentence one at a time. Ask whether it needs to exist: does it add something material that no other sentence in TARGET already says? Cut any sentence that only restates, previews or sums up what another sentence covers, however well it's written. When a sentence partly overlaps another, move what's new into the one you keep and cut the rest.

Then tighten the sentences that remain, focusing on:

- Condensing wordy passages
- Removing unnecessary filler
- Untangling convoluted or confusing phrasing

Preserve the core meaning, and keep every sentence complete and natural. Don't compress prose into fragments, drop articles or connecting words, or chop sentences short to save words. The goal is to cut repetition and convoluted phrasing, not to make the writing terse.
